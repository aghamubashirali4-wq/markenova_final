Industry Research

What Tech Startups Get Wrong About Marketing

Most startup marketing fails for the same reasons. After studying hundreds of SaaS launches, here's what separates the companies that scale from the ones that stall.

68%

Of SaaS landing pages fail in 5 seconds

The visitor's first 5 seconds decide if they stay or leave. Most SaaS landing pages lead with features instead of outcomes. We design for the 5-second test — clear value, clear ICP, clear next action.

4.2x

Higher conversion with ICP-specific pages

One-size-fits-all SaaS homepages convert 4.2x worse than landing pages built for specific ICPs. We build separate pages for each customer segment so each visitor sees their exact problem solved.

3-6 mo

Content SEO compound timeline

SaaS content SEO doesn't deliver leads in week one. But after 3-6 months of strategic content, it compounds into the cheapest, highest-converting channel in your stack. We build for compounding returns.

$2.50

Average CAC payback ratio

Healthy SaaS companies generate $2.50+ for every $1 of CAC. Most early-stage SaaS spend without tracking this metric. We build attribution systems from day one — even on shoestring budgets.

7x

Better trial-to-paid with onboarding emails

Free trial conversion is mostly determined by what happens AFTER signup. We build 5-7 email onboarding sequences that walk users to their "aha moment" — increasing trial-to-paid by 3-7x.

90%

Of startups skip retargeting

90% of trial signups don't convert in week one — but most startups have no retargeting in place. We build sequenced retargeting on Meta, LinkedIn, and Google that recovers lost trial signups.
