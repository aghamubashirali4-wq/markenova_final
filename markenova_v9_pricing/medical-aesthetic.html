Industry Research

What Medical & Aesthetic Marketing Gets Wrong

Patient acquisition isn't lead generation. The buying psychology, regulatory considerations, and lifetime value math all change. Here's what most agencies miss.

3.2x

More inquiries from single-service pages

Practice websites that build dedicated landing pages for each high-value service (Invisalign, Botox, Laser Hair Removal) get 3.2x more inquiries than generic "All Services" pages. We build out service-specific pages from day one.

87%

Of patients read reviews first

Before booking a medical or aesthetic appointment, 87% of patients read reviews. Most practices have under 25 Google reviews. We build review velocity systems that 5-10x your review count in 90 days.

$340

Average cost per dental patient

For practices NOT running optimized campaigns. We've seen this number drop to $80-120 with proper landing pages, conversion tracking, and audience targeting. The savings often fund the entire marketing budget.

68%

Don't track patient lifetime value

Most med spas and dental practices optimize for cost per lead — not cost per high-LTV patient. We integrate practice management data to optimize for the patients who book recurring high-value services.

5 sec

To convert mobile patient searchers

Mobile patients searching "med spa near me" abandon sites with poor first impressions in under 5 seconds. We build sites optimized for the moment of decision — visual, trust-heavy, and click-to-call ready.

4.2x

Higher ROI with retention funnels

Acquiring a new patient costs 4-7x more than retaining one. We build email and SMS retention sequences specific to aesthetic and dental practices — appointment reminders, treatment follow-ups, and rebooking offers.
