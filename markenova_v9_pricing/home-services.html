Industry Research

What Home Service Businesses Get Wrong Online

After analyzing hundreds of home service Google Ads accounts, websites, and Google Business Profiles, the same mistakes repeat across the industry. Here's what we see — and how we fix it.

73%

Wasted on broad keywords

Most home service Google Ads accounts bid on broad match keywords without negative keyword lists. The result: paying $30 per click for searches that will never convert. We tighten match types and build out negative keyword strategies in the first 14 days.

52%

Missing call tracking

Over half of home service businesses can't tell you which ads, keywords, or channels generated their last 10 service calls. Without call tracking, every optimization is a guess. We implement CallRail or equivalent on every account.

81%

Weak Google Business Profile

Most service businesses treat Google Business Profile as a one-time setup. The companies winning local rankings post weekly, respond to every review, add service area pages, and use GBP messaging. This alone can 2-3x local visibility.

9 sec

Average time on slow sites

Mobile homeowners searching "emergency plumber near me" abandon sites that take longer than 3 seconds to load. We rebuild on modern, fast infrastructure — Core Web Vitals green across the board.

68%

No service area pages

Single "Services" pages don't rank for "[service] in [city]" — and that's where the highest-intent traffic is. We build out individual service area pages targeting every city you serve.

94%

Don't retarget website visitors

Most home service customers compare 3-5 contractors before deciding. Without retargeting, you spend ad budget to send visitors to competitors. We set up Meta Pixel + Google retargeting on day one.
